## 1. What the 3 reference accounts teach us

| Account | Followers | Format | Best performers (plays) | Weak performers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **@vichardesk** | 23K | Faceless 2D doodle animation, Hinglish, ~45–55s | Japan-style election in India (13.3M), political news (4.8–4.9M), *Why Kumbhkaran slept so much* (1.7M), *How Laxman didn't sleep for 14 years* (159K) | Maratha empire history (4K), book gossip (6K) |
| **@sipandsips** | 59K | On camera, finance expert (CFP), ~50–70s | *Car buyers forget to claim TCS* (15M), *4 documents every 18+ must have* (14.7M), *Celeb wedding cost* (7.8M), *NVIDIA downfall* (476K) | Health insurance review (2.9K), digital blackout (3.1K), sponsored post (2.3K) |
| **@theindiantrivia** | 28K | On camera, mostly English, textbook GK | Ramsar sites–Iran link (17.6K), Vande Bharat regenerative braking (16.1K), 6 regions of India (11.8K) | Podcast clips (~1–3K each) |

### Patterns behind the hits
1. **A myth-busting hook beats a plain fact.** "Lakshman Rekha wasn't TRUE 😱" and "Ravan never kidnapped Sita?" get far more views than "What led to the fall of the Maratha Empire". Start each video with a belief and then challenge it.
2. **"Useful to me right now" content goes mega-viral.** The 15M and 14.7M finance reels give viewers an action that saves money or paperwork. Abstract finance topics flop.
3. **Hot news gives spikes, and evergreen series give a base.** VicharDesk's 13M hits came from politics, but its Ramayan series reliably gets 100K–1.7M.
4. **Animation lets you stay faceless and scale.** VicharDesk gets the most plays per follower of the three with a simple stick-figure style.
5. **Talking-head textbook GK and podcast clips perform worst.** TheIndianTrivia has the credibility but lacks hooks.

## 3. Recommended channel concept

**Format:** Hinglish, faceless (or semi-faceless), animated explainers with one recurring doodle character as the "host".

**Promise:** *"The story behind what every Indian thinks they know."*

### 3 content pillars (rotate them)
| Pillar | Share | Why | Example topics |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Mythology & History: myth vs. fact** | 40% | Biggest search demand, rides the Ramayana-movie trend, evergreen | Lakshman Rekha isn't in the Valmiki Ramayana; Why Kumbhkaran slept 6 months; Did Ravan really win Ayodhya?; Real history of the Kohinoor |
| **B. Paisa & System: money and paperwork hacks for Indians** | 35% | Highest CPM (ad revenue per view), proven 15M-scale virality | TCS refund when buying a car; 4 documents every 18-year-old needs; Check your EPF on WhatsApp; Is your gold really pure? (HUID check) |

> Keep politics **explanatory, not opinion**. VicharDesk's political reels go viral, but they also bring channel-strike and defamation risk.

## 4. Format and publishing schedule

| Format | Length | Frequency | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Shorts** | 45–60s | 5 per week (1 per weekday) | Discovery and subscriber growth |
| **Long-form** | 8–12 min | 1 per week (Saturday) | Watch hours, ad revenue, loyalty |
| **Community post / poll** | – | 2 per week | "Which myth should we bust next?" |

**Repurposing loop:** each long video becomes 3–4 Shorts, and each Short is also posted as an Instagram Reel with the same hook.

### Script template for a Short (~130 words)
1. **0–3s hook:** challenge a belief. *"Lakshman Rekha… Valmiki Ramayan mein hai hi nahi!"*
2. **3–10s stakes:** *"Toh phir yeh kahani aayi kahan se?"*
3. **10–45s payoff:** 3 quick beats with one visual per beat.
4. **45–55s twist or CTA:** *"Part 2 mein — Ravan ne Sita ko kaise uthaya? Follow karo."*

### Long-form structure
Cold-open hook (30s), then the context, then 3–5 chapters, then a surprising conclusion, then a teaser for the next video. Add chapters and a sources list to the description.

| Need | Free / cheap option |
|---|---|
| Script research | Primary sources (Valmiki Ramayan translations, PIB, RBI, Income Tax site) plus your own notes |

**Time per Short:** about 2–3 hours at first, falling to about 1 hour with templates. **Per long video:** about 8–12 hours.

- [ ] Choose the name and secure the YouTube, Instagram and email handles
- [ ] Design the character, logo, banner and a thumbnail template
- [ ] Write the channel description with keywords: *India explained, Ramayan facts, history in Hindi, money tips India*

- [ ] Turn the best-performing Short topics into long videos

## 8. Risks and rules to follow
- **Finance (SEBI finfluencer rules):** give education only. Don't recommend specific stocks or funds unless you are SEBI-registered. Add a disclaimer.
- **Mythology:** be respectful. Name the version you're citing ("Valmiki says X, Tulsidas says Y"). This avoids backlash and is more interesting.
- **Politics and news:** state facts with sources and avoid personal attacks. Clickbait about named people can bring legal notices.
- **Copyright:** don't use movie or TV clips (sipandsips uses credited clips, which is risky). Your own animation avoids this.
- **Originality:** never copy reference creators' scripts or art style. Take the format lessons, not the content.
